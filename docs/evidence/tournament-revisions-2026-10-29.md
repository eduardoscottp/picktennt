# Saved tournament revisions and fair scheduling

## Delivered locally

- Administration offers setup/rules edits, full graph edits, approved team names, batch player swaps/replacements using existing profiles, explicit removals including pending entries, and singles/doubles conversion.
- Every structural revision requires a reason and current impact preview. Confirming creates a new published definition and stage instances, preserves previous rows, deactivates previous fixtures, and closes registration. It never generates new games automatically.
- Reopen registration to add more accounts. Review actual group assignments and the concrete schedule, then confirm generation. Capacity reduction cannot silently remove registered entries. Unsupported or dangling advancement references are rejected.
- Any historical start, score or outcome locks structural revision. Published event name and venue description remain editable separately; existing court moves and result-correction workflows remain available.
- Revisioned administrator admissions preserve original personal-consent evidence. Pending members can accept the current rules again; swapping back reuses membership rows safely.
- Circle-method matchups are retained. Global deterministic court allocation interleaves independent groups and prioritizes waiting players. In the six-team/two-court regression, the original split-circle schedule uses 10 slots with a maximum two-slot internal wait; the new schedule uses 8 slots with a maximum one-slot wait. Equal real playing minutes or equal game counts across different group sizes are not promised.
- Existing persisted rounds and courts stay pinned when new scores resolve participants, including tournaments generated by the prior scheduler.
- Web and MCP expose revision/schedule previews; exact schedule preview hashes reproduce the reviewed draw and reject changed inputs. Existing command/receipt concurrency and retry protections remain intact.
