## 3. Information we collect

**Account and profile information.** Your name, email address, username, account identifier, and profile information you provide. We also record the version and time of your acceptance of the Terms of Use and acknowledgment of this Policy.

**Match (game) information.** Results, match events, player slots, and related information you record in the App.

**Activity and health information (via Apple HealthKit).** With your permission, the App reads information such as heart rate, active energy, steps, and workout data in order to show you metrics for your matches. This health information is processed locally on your device and Apple Watch and is **not** transmitted to our servers. Deleting the App removes its local copy on that device; it does not delete records already saved in Apple Health or copies on your other devices. See Section 11 for details.

**Technical and diagnostic information.** The initial iPhone and Apple Watch release does not enable third-party in-App analytics or crash reporting. Our authentication and hosting services may process operational information, such as network requests and security logs, to operate and protect the service.

**Support information.** If you contact us, we process the information you provide in order to respond.

## 4. How your information is stored and transmitted

Account information is processed by our authentication service. Game information is stored on your devices and synced to our servers to back up your history and restore it to your account. Health information remains on your device and Apple Watch and is not sent to our servers (see Section 11). Where information is transmitted to our servers, it is protected in transit using HTTPS/TLS encryption and encrypted at rest under our infrastructure provider’s standard configuration. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

**Health and fitness information is excluded.** We do not use health or fitness information for marketing or advertising, regardless of its source. The analytics, model-training, audience, advertising, and third-party-sharing purposes above apply **only** to non-health game and account information. **Health information is never used for advertising, marketing, or audiences and is never shared with third parties**, consistent with Apple App Review Guideline 5.1.3.

## 11. Health information (Apple HealthKit)

This Section describes how we handle health and physical-activity information and forms part of this Policy.

**What we access.** With your permission, the App reads heart rate, active energy (calories), steps, and workout information through Apple HealthKit to show you metrics for your matches.

**Where it is processed.** Health information is processed and stored **only on your device and Apple Watch and is not transmitted to our servers**. Your Apple Watch may sync a match to your own iPhone through system features, but health information does not leave your devices for Idddeas. Deleting the App removes its local copy on that device; it does not delete records already saved in Apple Health or copies on your other devices.

**How we use it, and what we never do.** Health information is used **only** on your device to show you your own metrics. We do **not** use it for advertising, marketing, audiences, or model training; we do **not** sell it; and we do **not** share it with third parties. This exclusion is unconditional, consistent with Apple Guideline 5.1.3. The advertising and sharing activities described in Sections 5 and 7 apply only to game and account information—never to health information.

**Your control.** You can grant or revoke the App’s HealthKit access at any time in your device settings. With your permission, Picktennt can save a completed pickleball workout to Apple Health when you choose to save it. Health access is optional and separate from accepting the Terms or creating an account. You can use the scoreboard without granting Health access.

**Your responsibility for locally stored information.** Because this information remains on your device and within your control, you are responsible for protecting your device—through a passcode, biometrics, device encryption, backups, and control over who can access it. We do not receive this information, do not store it on our servers, and are not responsible for its safekeeping, loss, or unauthorized access on your device. This does not limit any mandatory consumer rights you may have.

**Regulatory note.** Because health information does not leave your device, its regulatory footprint is reduced; even so, specific consumer-health-data laws may apply depending on your market.

**Cloud backup.** Game-history backup is already available. HealthKit-derived heart-rate, calorie, step, and workout-summary fields are excluded from the server sync.
